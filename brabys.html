02 / Strategic Brand Concept

Braby's: The first baby friendly restaurant in the world

Martina Solés Caldés • September 2026

01. Insights

  • By 2026, Baby-Led Weaning (BLW) has established itself across social media as the ultimate method for raising more independent babies, surpassing traditional purees.
  • Parents want to do their best for their children and follow current recommendations, but the process is stressful, messy, and causes anxiety (fear of choking, lack of suitable spaces outside the home).
  • Key Insight: Parents want to do it, but they are afraid; Braby's resolves this friction by turning the desire into a safe, low-pressure environment.

02. Concept

The world's first restaurant truly tailored for babies (6 to 18 months) and their caregivers. Spaces and food designed for every developmental stage, staffed by trained personnel to prevent accidents and eliminate the stress of dining out.

03. Brand Identity

Naming: Baby + Brave = Braby's (alludes to the courage needed to try something new with a child and for a baby to self-feed). The apostrophe "s" reinforces that it is THE place belonging to babies.

Typography & Style: Logotype in a handwritten script typeface with a slight wobble (infantile drawing style). Headlines set in Shortcake and body copy in Ruluko.

Color Palette: Primary orange (warm, appetizing, and safe #F97D50), secondary blue and yellow (#4949C2, #FDAF2E), and neutral background tones (#EBE5DA).

System: Friendly illustrated characters (broccoli, lion, cloud, etc.) unifying all touchpoints (menus, stickers, aprons, walls).

04. Audience & Positioning

Audience

Urban parents in Barcelona, approximately 28 to 42 years old, time-poor, accustomed to dining out, and determined not to give up their lifestyle due to the arrival of a 6 to 18-month-old baby.

Positioning

While traditional family-friendly restaurants offer space for older children (and unhealthy food) and BLW guides remain stuck in digital theory, Braby's is the first physical space that brings solid feeding into practical reality with support and zero judgment.

05. Brand Pillars & Experience

Mess is anticipated and managed; no apologies needed.
Menu structured by feeding stages accompanied by trained staff.
The same high-quality food for everyone at the same table.
Taking home dishes, routine, and confidence.

Experience & Space: Fresh and organic food cooked daily, stroller parking, ball pit, play area, fully equipped changing rooms, integrated shop on-site, and age-adapted menus.

06. Vision

Success is measured by a parent leaving less anxious than when they walked in, returning for their baby's next developmental stage.
