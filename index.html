About me

I've spent 8 years designing digital products across hospitality SaaS, AI tools, crypto and automotive.

I'm used to being the only designer on a product team, working closely with PMs and engineers, and I've also built and led design teams from scratch. I like messy problems, clear flows and products that hold up in real life, whether that's a hotel lobby at check-in time or a phone on a train. Based in Barcelona, working remotely in English and Spanish.

What I do: end-to-end product design, user research and usability testing, UX/UI for web and mobile, design systems, accessibility, data-informed iteration and design leadership.
