Sunsies Studio Branding

Identity Design, Brand Narrative & Creative Direction

01. Origin & Strategy

Sunsies Studio originated as a personal exploration project that evolved organically into a specialized brand focused on warm-toned home decor.

The strategic approach prioritized building a solid visual narrative and a direct emotional connection with the audience, demonstrating the ability to craft complete brand universes from scratch.

02. Visual Identity & Brand System

Friendly Visual Language

The identity relies on rounded, organic shapes and a playful yet refined approach, moving away from rigid codes to convey closeness and distinct personality.

Color Palette & Warm Tones

Construction of a chromatic universe based on pastel tones that bring calm, luminosity, and visual coherence across all brand touchpoints.
